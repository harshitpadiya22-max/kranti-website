Kranti Launches National Passion-Based Career Platform

Official Announcement • January 2026

Kranti officially announces the launch of its national platform focused on passion-based career development for youth across India.

The initiative aims to recognize hidden passions, organize youth into purpose-driven communities, and create structured pathways for careers beyond traditional models.

Kranti operates as a parallel civic–economic ecosystem where youth are not members, but contributors, leaders, and decision-makers.

Further announcements regarding mentorship programs, community councils, and national initiatives will follow.
